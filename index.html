Automating knowledge ingestion for AI agents

The challenge Keep agent knowledge up to date without relying on manual document onboarding or a separate re-indexing step.

What I worked on Designed a consumer-driven pipeline where Kafka events trigger document processing. The service parses content, creates chunks, generates embeddings and writes them to vector storage for retrieval. The ingestion service operates independently from the query-time retrieval API.

Why it matters Document updates become repeatable background workflows, while retrieval remains focused on serving low-latency queries.
